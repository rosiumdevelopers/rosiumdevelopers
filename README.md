## What to Look for in a Real Estate Developer

Before selecting a property developer, consider the following factors:

### 1. Project Location

A suitable location should offer convenient access to important roads and everyday facilities. Consider the distance from schools, hospitals, markets and workplaces according to your requirements.

### 2. Project Planning

Review the layout plan, plot dimensions, internal roads and available infrastructure. A properly planned development can make it easier to understand how the property is organised.

### 3. Legal Approvals and Documentation

Check the approved layout, ownership records, applicable authority permissions and RERA registration details wherever required. Always verify the documents independently before booking.

### 4. Transparent Information

A property developer should clearly explain plot availability, pricing, payment schedules, additional charges and the terms mentioned in the agreement.

### 5. Site Visit and Communication

Visit the development personally and ask questions about the project. Clear communication can help you understand the property and avoid misunderstandings during the buying process.

## Why Visit a Property Before Making a Decision?

Online project information is useful for creating an initial shortlist, but a physical site visit offers a better understanding of the development.

During your visit, check the approach road, surrounding properties, plot position, internal infrastructure and nearby facilities. You can also discuss the available plot sizes, payment terms and documentation with the project representative.

Avoid relying only on promotional claims or expected future development. Make your decision after reviewing the actual property and its relevant documents.

Ask for a written breakdown of the total cost and read the agreement carefully. If you are unfamiliar with property documentation, consult a qualified legal professional before signing.
